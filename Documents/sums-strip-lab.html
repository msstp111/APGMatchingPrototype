The sums strip — ten ideas against what ships today

The subject is the block at the top of the expanded card and nothing else; the row above it and the bands below are context, so the strip is judged where it lives. The complaint: the two left labels wrap to two lines and the right one does not, so the block is lopsided and its height is set by the longest caption rather than by anything being read. The repeated words are what make those captions long — QUANTITY MATCHED is printed twice and QUANTITY three times — and 30 drafted is the only figure up there with no caption of its own, while being the one the Confirm space gate turns on. The ideas are layouts, so one at a time; the five modifiers compose with all of them. Keys 1–9 0 pick an idea, B the baseline, 6 pressed again gives 6b, R A F D E toggle the modifiers, [ ] step the record and Q cycles the quantity state — under, exact, over — which is what the strip looks like on a card that is perfectly matched or over matched.

The diagnosis

  1. The lopsidedness is the symptom. Two two-line labels beside a one-line label, so the strip's height comes from its longest caption.
  2. The repeated words are the cause. Nothing in the strip is about anything but this record's quantity, so QUANTITY earns its keep once at most — and QUANTITY MATCHED is what makes the two left captions too long for one line.
  3. One figure has no caption. 30 drafted rides on the incl-Draft numeral, and it is the figure the confirm gate turns on.
  4. Height is not the problem. Two attempts on 2026-09-08 spent the drawer's width instead and both were worse — see the foot of this panel.

Modifiers

The three states

State in the bar swaps the four figures, the state word, the ramp colour and the match list, and keeps the record's identity — so the strip is the only thing that changes.
  1. Under — a positive remainder in the ramp's orange. The ordinary case, and the one every layout here was drawn against.
  2. Exact — 0 in green, and 77 of 77 on both sums. This is the case the fraction earns its keep on: a bare 77 said nothing about being finished, and 77 of 77 says it without a word.
  3. Over — a negative figure, and the sums exceed their own denominator (86 of 77). On a space it is blue: over-filling is permitted and expected. On an availability record it is pink — over-committing supply should be impossible, so the colour is a bug flag rather than a description. Switch to the Supply record with the same state on to see the two side by side.
The meter above the drawer moves too: past 100% it cannot grow, so both segments pin at the track's end and an over-run cap appears past its right edge (design-system.md §4.2).

Two layouts were tried and rejected on 2026-09-08, both recorded in design-system.md §6.2 item 1: one row with the prose leading, which wrapped and dropped the figures below the notes; and all four figures on the trailing edge, which read as the fragment of a row rather than as the drawer's head. Neither is repeated here.
