The diagnosis

  1. The value range is exhausted. Eight named greys inside ten L* points. A ninth step separates nothing — what is left is removing a grey, or a device that is not a value.
  2. Band count, not band colour. Five horizontal grounds per open card; three open is fifteen.
  3. Nothing says which card owns which drawer. Only adjacency does — the one signal that fails when adjacency repeats.
