The blue ladder — where the open card's rail goes once the bands are blue

Left is the screen as it was before 2026-09-10: grey bands, one tinted current week, and an open card whose rail was #DDEAF1 — a value chosen precisely so it would not equal the current-week band. Items 7 and 8 took that argument away by tinting every band.

Settled 2026-09-11, and the answer was a round trip. Solid petrol (option 2) shipped from this file on the 10th and was reverted the next day: on screen it was far too heavy for what it says, since an open card is the most ordinary state in the list. The rail is #DDEAF1 again — option 4 — with the bands blue around it, which is what the right board now opens on. So the collision the original token warned about is live and accepted: what separates an open rail from a drop target is shape, not value.

The diagnosis

  1. The ladder is full. Five petrol values, all spoken for: #F6FAFC column wash, #F5F9FB hover, #E8F1F6 this week, #DDEAF1 open, #D1E1E8 droppable. Item 7 takes the third for every band and item 8 needs a sixth below it.
  2. The rail's own argument assumed one tinted band. _lms-tokens.scss:46-48 rejects the tint because “a card inside the current week would have a grip the same colour as the header directly above it”. Tint every band and that becomes every card, in every week — so the token's reasoning does not survive item 7 whatever else happens.
  3. The current week's ground is not carrying the distinction alone. It also has a 2px petrol top rule where every other band has 2px #757575, a petrol bold label, and a This week tag. Item 8's ground reinforces three existing channels rather than being the only one — which is what makes a subtler answer viable.
  4. Past versus future is the quiet casualty. Today past and future bands differ by ground as well as by label ink and the Past tag. Item 7 makes every non-current band one colour, so only ink and the tag are left. Look at the top band against the bottom one before deciding that is acceptable.
  5. The drop target must not become the current week. #D1E1E8 exists because at one strength “a target three rows down and ‘this is the week you are in’ were indistinguishable at a glance”. Item 8's darker blue recreates exactly that collision unless it clears #D1E1E8. Turn on Adjacency.
