Where the status goes

Today the status word is the last item in .trail, and the trail is flush with the card's trailing edge — so Booked ends at x=468, which is where the meter's numeral ends and where the UNMATCHED heading ends. Reading down that column gives Unmatched → 180 → Booked. It is also ragged on its other side: the match count in front of it varies by ~50px, so the status glyph starts at a different x on nearly every row.

Line 2 of the card grid places three things — .meta in column 1, .month in column 3, .trail across 3–6. Grid area 2 / 2 is empty, and has been since the two lines became one grid. It is 96px wide, it already sits under a heading, and a status at its longest measures 69px. Every option below is judged on the readout, not the eye.
