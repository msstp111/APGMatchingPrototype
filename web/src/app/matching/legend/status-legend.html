What the cards are telling you

Two things are being said at once, and each has its own channel so they never compete: the left edge and the icon carry status, never colour; the meter carries quantity, never pattern.

  • A Processor Space is never Pending. A space with nothing but drafted matches against it stays Booked, so it stays inside the default filter and does not quietly disappear from the column while you are working on it.
  • Cancelled is the one status that changes the whole card, and it does it by taking colour away rather than adding any.

  • Orange and green mean the same thing on both columns. Only the over state differs, because over-filling a processor's space is normal and over-committing a farmer's stock is not.
  • A Livestock Availability record says Under-committed and Fully committed where a Processor Space says Under-filled and Filled, the same two colours, the words that suit the side.

The red badge: a match whose other half was cancelled

block space cancelled In the match table Cancelling a record never cancels its matches. That is deliberate, so APG can line up an alternative before anyone is told their booking has gone. The match stays live and still holds stock on this card, and the badge names the side that has gone. Cancel the match itself from the match window when you are ready.
expand_more On a collapsed card The same thing said in the only space a 52px row has left: a red square behind the expand chevron. Open the card and the match table names which match it is.

Red is the one place on this screen where colour is not a quantity. It is allowed because it is not reporting a status (the card's own status is untouched); it is flagging work outstanding.

The two columns do not share a stock-class list, and nothing on this screen tries to match them up for you. Judging whether a class on one side suits a class on the other is the job you are here to do, so the stock class is spelled out in full on every card rather than abbreviated to a symbol.
