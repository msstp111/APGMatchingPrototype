Reset the demo data?

warning
This cannot be undone. The database is dropped and re-seeded from scratch, and the page reloads.

What goes:

  • Every match drafted, edited, confirmed or cancelled since the last reset.
  • Every record added, edited or cancelled from the demo tools.
  • Both columns' filters and sort, the column order, and any expanded cards.

What comes back is the seeded demo set, identical every time, including the worked examples the walkthrough in Documents/DEMO.md depends on.
