APG matching — drop-target interaction lab

# THROWAWAY # A standalone page for feeling twelve alternative drop-target treatments against the matching screen's real geometry and palette. It is not part of the Angular application, is not routed, imports nothing and is imported by nothing. Drag a card from either column onto a card in the other; every toggle off is today's behaviour exactly, so it is the control.

The diagnosis: the app has no “under the pointer” state at all, so eligibility highlighting is doing a job it cannot do — and its petrol tint collides with the current-week band, which wears the same colour for an unrelated reason.

Twelve ideas

Dropping creates nothing. There is no API, no state and no match — a valid drop shows a toast naming the two records and the head count it would have proposed, and that is all. Escape cancels a drag in flight, as it does in the app.

With everything off the source row is left completely untouched while its copy is on the pointer. That is not an omission here — it is what the app does today. Idea 12 is the one declaration that changes it.

Recommended and Paired are unchanged and still ship 8. My read is that both are better with 8b in its place — it frees half a row without leaving the column grid — but the presets are yours to move, not mine: tick 8b over either of them and see. Everything ticks the twelve numbered ideas; the two variants, 8b and 12b, are left to you.
