The open row — ten ways to make an expanded card read as open

Both boards show the same column with one card open. The left is what ships today: the drawer carries a frame, a rail, a notch and the screen's only shadow, and the 52px row above it carries nothing at all — .card gets no open-state class, and the only thing that changes when a card opens is that its chevron flips. That row is the click target for closing it and the drop target for matching onto it, which is why a reviewer called the open card unclear. The right board takes any combination of the ten. Ideas 3 and 4 shipped on 2026-09-09 — press SHIPPED for them, and note the left board is now the screen as it was before that, not as it is. Click a note to toggle it, or press 1–9 and 0. Use Open row to put the treatment on each status in turn — Booked's spine is 3px where every other status has 6, and Cancelled greyscales the row, which takes the petrol out of anything that put petrol on it. Stripe lands the open row on white or on #F5F5F5. Hover pins the real hover state next to the open row, or on it. Drag paints the Phase 9 drop state on the card below. The hash carries the set.

The diagnosis

  1. The row has no open state. Not a wrong choice — a gap. All four devices went to the sheet; the row kept its chevron rotation and nothing else.
  2. Value is still exhausted. Eight named greys inside ten L* points, two since deleted. A ninth step separates nothing, so what is left is geometry, continuity, or an existing element changing state.
  3. Hue is committed to the quantity meter, and petrol is spent twice in the list already — #E8F1F6 is this week, #D1E1E8 is the card under a drag.
  4. Cancelled breaks the continuity ideas. cancelled-card greyscales .card and not the drawer, so 3 and 5 lose their colour on the row while the sheet keeps it. Found by drawing it, not by reasoning about it.
  5. Hover cannot do it alone. #F5F9FB is ~1.4 L* off the zebra stripe and ~2.4 off white. Hover reads because it arrives; a static 1.4 L* somewhere in forty rows is not findable. Set Hover to “the open row” and see.
